Questions

Before you reach out.

Anything else? Write to gapleeco@gmail.com.

What's in the free preview?

A quick concept of your site, built with your brand, so you can see it before paying anything. There's no commitment if it isn't right for you.

How does payment work?

The scope and price are fixed in the proposal before any build starts, and they don't change. You pay 50% to start and 50% at launch.

What if I want changes?

Two rounds of revisions are included in every build. After that, we launch it on your own domain.

Do you look after the site after launch?

If you'd like. The optional care plan covers monthly hosting and content updates.
