Data & Privacy

Your data stays yours.

SkillGap is built so we never hold onto your student data. We don't store, profile, or keep student records, grades, or personal information. The product simply doesn't work that way.

If we plug into your existing system, assessment data flows through your platform. We receive only what's needed to generate feedback (performance, topic, timing) and return the result. Nothing identifying stays with us.

If we build a system for you, the interface, your branding, and your data are yours. The SkillGap intelligence that powers the feedback runs as a service we host and you license, so you never have to maintain our engine yourself.

What we can see
Only what's needed for feedback
We work with the performance data required to generate a result, and we don't keep it afterward. No student records, grades, or personal details live on our side.
No lock-in, ever
Stay because it works, not because you're stuck
No multi-year contracts: your license is yours to cancel anytime. Leave whenever and take all your data with you, and your own system keeps running. The SkillGap feedback layer is the one service you license, so cancelling simply stops new AI feedback. Nothing is ever held hostage.
Compliance & Billing

Paperwork handled, wherever you are.

Tax and compliance rules differ from country to country. Wherever your institution is based, we take care of our side and give your finance team exactly the documentation they need: proper invoices, the right tax references, and any certificates your procurement process asks for.

Before anything is signed, we'll lay out clearly what applies to your institution. No guesswork, no surprises.

Simple on your end
Tax is settled the standard way
In most countries, business-to-business digital services are handled so any local tax is settled by your institution at the usual rate. Your invoice from us arrives clean, at the agreed amount, and your accounting team handles the rest the way they already do.
Public institutions
Built for public procurement
Public and state institutions often have strict purchasing rules. We provide the documentation your procurement office needs to move forward through the proper channels, and we'll point you to exactly what's required for your country.
Need something specific for your finance or legal team, like a particular invoice format, a tax certificate, or proof of registration? Just ask. We'll prepare whatever your institution's process requires.
Payment

Payment that fits your cycle.

Payments are made by international bank transfer or Wise, and invoices can be issued in your preferred currency. The annual per-student license is billed once per year, so there is nothing to meter and no surprise top-ups.

For institutions on fixed budget-release cycles, we're happy to work to a milestone-based payment schedule on custom builds. The scope and schedule are agreed in writing before any work begins, so everyone knows exactly what to expect.

FAQ

Common questions.

What happens to our data if we stop using SkillGap?
If we're plugged into your system, there's nothing to hand back, since your data was never on our side. If we built a system for you, it's already fully yours. Either way, ending things has zero impact on your data or your students.
Who handles the tax on our payments?
It depends on your country, but in most B2B cases your institution settles any local tax in the normal way and pays us the agreed amount. We provide compliant invoices and the right references for your records, and we'll confirm exactly how it works for your jurisdiction before you sign.
Can a public or state institution buy without a full bidding process?
Often, yes. Many public institutions can purchase proprietary software directly when competitive sourcing isn't practical. We provide the supporting documentation your procurement office needs to take through the proper channels, and we'll tell you what applies in your country.
What documentation can you provide for our finance and legal teams?
Whatever your process requires: compliant invoices, tax references, proof of registration, and eligibility certificates for procurement. Tell us what your team needs and we'll have it ready before your first call.
How does the licensing term work if we leave early?
The per-student license is an annual term with no multi-year lock-in, so you can cancel at renewal. The license covers your enrolled students for the year it's paid for, and there's nothing to meter or use up. If you wind down mid-term, your data and your own system are unaffected, since only the SkillGap feedback layer is the licensed service.
