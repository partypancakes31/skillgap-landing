The model

One price per enrolled student, per year.

Every student is covered for the year, with a generous feedback allowance built in, so there's nothing to meter and no surprise bills. Pick the AI tier that fits your priorities.

Common questions

Pricing, answered.

Why per student instead of per use?
Because it's predictable. Metered usage means a bill that swings with how many assessments you run, which is a forecasting headache for any institution. A flat per-student license is one clean annual number you can budget against, with the usage allowance generous enough that you never think about it.
What if our enrolment grows next year?
The license tracks enrolment, so it adjusts at renewal. There's no penalty and no renegotiation mid-year. As you grow into the larger bands, volume discounts bring the per-student rate down.
Is the Integration Assessment really free for founding partners?
Yes. While we onboard our first partner institutions, the assessment is waived and pilots run at 50%. For everyone after that, it starts from $750 and is credited toward the build if you proceed, so it's effectively free the moment you move forward.
Can pricing fit our budget-release cycle?
Yes. Licensing is billed annually, and for custom builds with public budget cycles we can structure milestone-based payments. The scope and schedule are agreed in writing before any work begins.
Where are the exact figures for our institution?
The per-student rates above are firm. Your setup and any volume discount are scoped to your specific size and system, so the exact numbers come with your quote after a short conversation. No pressure, no obligation.
