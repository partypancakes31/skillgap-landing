Questions

The things institutions ask first.

We already have an LMS. Why doesn't it do this?
Because an LMS is built to organize and deliver learning, not to diagnose it. Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, and every other platform store your assessment data but do not interpret it at the student level. No LMS has built this natively. SkillGap is the intelligence layer that sits on top of whatever you already have and turns that stored data into something students and faculty can actually act on. That is exactly the gap it fills.
Do we have to replace our current system?
No. SkillGap layers on top of your existing LMS or assessment platform through its API. Your students, logins, and question bank stay where they are; we just add the scoring and AI feedback. If you have no system yet, we can build one for you instead.
What if our system doesn't have everything SkillGap needs?
That's exactly what the upfront audit is for. SkillGap works from whatever data you already have: at minimum a student, a topic, and a score, which nearly every system stores. The more it can see (per-question answers, topic tags, timing), the more detailed the feedback gets, but it never simply fails because a field is missing. Anything genuinely absent we either capture through our own integration layer or scope into setup, and we'll show you exactly where you stand before you commit to anything.
How long until we're up and running?
A pilot can be live within one assessment cycle. We start with an audit, connect or build, then run a single class end to end before any wider rollout. No multi-year IT project.
Will this replace our teachers?
No. SkillGap does the repetitive diagnostic work, not the teaching. Faculty keep full control over grades and judgment, every AI result is reviewable, and feedback is always labeled as machine-generated.
What subjects does it support?
Any subject. The three-factor scoring and AI feedback work across disciplines, from programming to nursing to business. Java Programming is just the example shown in our demo.
Is our student data safe?
Yes. We only use the performance data needed to generate feedback, and we don't hold onto it. Compliance depends on your country, and we'll walk you through exactly what applies before anything is signed. See procurement & compliance.
What does it cost?
Simple annual licensing, priced per enrolled student (from $12/student/year), with a generous feedback allowance built in so there's nothing to meter. Getting started is a one-time Integration Assessment, which is free for founding partners and credited toward your build. See pricing.
